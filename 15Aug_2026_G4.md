# 🇮🇳 Grade 4 Independence Day Performance
# Unity in Diversity – We Build India Together

## Overview

This is a simple, practical, and engaging musical mime performance for **Grade 4** students that can be prepared within **6 days**.

The performance focuses on the theme **"Unity in Diversity"**, showing how people from different professions work together to build our nation.

- **Duration:** 4–5 minutes
- **Students:** 10 (5 pairs)
- **Dialogues:** Only narrator
- **Student Dialogues:** None
- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **Preparation Time:** 6 Days

---

# Stage Setup

- Indian Flag at center stage
- Bluetooth speaker
- Wireless microphone (for narrator)
- Simple backdrop (optional)
- Tricolour decorations
- Five small props

---

# Roles

| Pair | Profession | Props |
|------|------------|-------|
| Pair 1 | Farmers | Toy sickle, basket, fake vegetables |
| Pair 2 | Soldiers | Cap, toy binoculars |
| Pair 3 | Doctors | Toy stethoscope, first-aid box |
| Pair 4 | Teachers | Book, chalk, pointer |
| Pair 5 | Scientists | White coat, goggles, notebook |

---

# Narrator Script

## Opening

> Good morning Respected Principal, teachers, parents and my dear friends.
>
> India is a land of many languages, cultures and traditions.
>
> Yet, we are one family.
>
> Today, the students of Grade Four proudly present...
>
> **"Unity in Diversity – We Build India Together."**

(Start the background music.)

---

# Scene 1 – Farmers

### Narrator

> Every grain of food begins with the hard work of our farmers.

### Actions

**Student 1**

- Dig the soil
- Sow seeds
- Wipe sweat

**Student 2**

- Water crops
- Harvest crops
- Lift basket proudly

### Ending Pose

Both students:

- Raise the basket
- Smile proudly
- Freeze for 5 seconds
- Move to the left side of the stage

---

# Scene 2 – Soldiers

### Narrator

> Our brave soldiers protect our nation day and night.

### Actions

**Student 1**

- Salute
- Look through binoculars
- March proudly

**Student 2**

- Stand guard
- Wave the Indian Flag
- Salute

### Ending Pose

- Freeze
- Join beside the farmers

---

# Scene 3 – Doctors

### Narrator

> Doctors care for us with kindness and dedication.

### Actions

- Check heartbeat
- Apply bandage
- Give medicine
- Smile

### Ending Pose

- Show thumbs-up
- Freeze
- Join previous group

---

# Scene 4 – Teachers

### Narrator

> Teachers guide us with knowledge and values.

### Actions

- Write on imaginary board
- Read a book
- Teach students
- Point toward the future

### Ending Pose

- Freeze
- Join others

---

# Scene 5 – Scientists

### Narrator

> Scientists help India grow with innovation and discovery.

### Actions

- Observe experiment
- Write notes
- Look through microscope
- Point proudly toward the sky

### Ending Pose

- Freeze
- Join everyone

---

# Grand Finale

### Narrator

> Different professions...
>
> Different talents...
>
> One Nation...
>
> One Dream...
>
> Together...
>
> We make India strong.

---

# Final Formation

- All students join hands.
- Form a semicircle.
- One student walks to the center holding the Indian Flag.

---

# Final Slogan

All students together:

> **Jai Hind!**

(Pause)

Then:

> **Vande Mataram!**

Hold the pose while the ending music continues.

Curtain closes.

---

# Director's Notes

## Farmers

- Work slowly.
- Smile while harvesting.

---

## Soldiers

- Walk confidently.
- Keep your back straight.
- No smiling or laughing.

---

## Doctors

- Show kindness.
- Pretend the patient feels better.

---

## Teachers

- Use large hand movements.
- Act naturally.

---

## Scientists

- Show curiosity.
- Pretend you made an important discovery.

---

# Stage Entry Plan

Do **not** keep everyone on stage from the beginning.

Instead:

1. Keep all pairs backstage.
2. Narrator introduces each profession.
3. Pair enters.
4. Performs actions.
5. Holds pose.
6. Moves to the side.
7. Next pair enters.

This creates a much more professional performance.

# Practice Schedule (6 Days)

| Day | Task |
|------|------|
| Day 1 | Assign roles and explain the story. Teach entry and exit. |
| Day 2 | Practice each pair separately until actions become natural. |
| Day 3 | Combine all scenes with narrator and background music. |
| Day 4 | Add props, stage positions, and final formation. |
| Day 5 | Full rehearsal with costumes (or props if costumes are unavailable). Correct expressions and timing. |
| Day 6 | Final dress rehearsal on the actual stage with music and microphone. |

---

# Optional Enhancement (Highly Recommended)

To make the performance more meaningful, let each pair also represent a different region of India through simple costume touches.

Example:

- Farmer – Punjab
- Soldier – Jammu & Kashmir
- Doctor – Kerala
- Teacher – Rajasthan
- Scientist – Tamil Nadu or Karnataka

Without adding any extra dialogue, the audience will understand that **people from different regions, cultures, and professions come together to build one India.**

This beautifully reinforces the theme:

> **Unity in Diversity**
>
> **"Many Cultures, One Nation."**
